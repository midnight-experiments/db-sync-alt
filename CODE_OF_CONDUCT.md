# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone taking part in the project's spaces: issues, pull
requests, discussions and reviews, and to anyone representing the project
elsewhere.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the
work. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing
someone's private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately: to the maintainer,
[@acedward](https://github.com/acedward), or through the repository's
[private reporting](https://github.com/midnight-experiments/db-sync-alt/security/advisories/new).
Reports are handled confidentially.
The maintainer decides on consequences as described in the Contributor
Covenant's enforcement guidelines, from a private warning to a permanent ban.
